v2.3.3 focused UI correction
----------------------------
- The per-product fast-live refresh indicator in VERBINDUNGS-PIDs is now drawn
  in the same vertical band as the PID/role text.
- The previous geometry at r.Bottom-7..r.Bottom-3 is removed; the track is
  centered around (r.Top+r.Bottom)/2 and therefore no longer appears as a second
  visual line below the connection text.
- PID/role text stays muted while refreshing; the Acid-Green segment is painted
  afterwards as the overlay. After completion, the overlay disappears and the
  verified live PID plus role label use the existing Acid-Green state.
- No live-probe, product identity, guided-learning, Gate 4, Health, Repair or
  persistence semantics are changed by this patch.
