v2.3.7 Healthcheck participation / Guided UI
---------------------------------------------
- SETUP column PROFILSTATUS is replaced by HEALTHCHECK.
- Clicking an available product row toggles only that product's Healthcheck
  participation. Active glyph/status are Acid Green; inactive glyph/status are
  muted grey. The product name and live connection presentation retain their own
  independent semantics.
- The participation choice is persisted by productKey in the app-owned schema-2
  inventory. Restart restores it. A later full Setup scan merges previous choices
  for recognized products; newly discovered products retain scanner default=true.
- Toggling participation immediately invalidates the current dashboard result to
  UNGEPRÜFT. Historical measurement files are not deleted.
- With zero active products, Setup scan and Healthcheck start are rejected before
  executing their scan/engine paths and an in-window dark app modal explains that
  at least one product must be active.
- Health Engine 1.4.4 derives required PIDs and relevant connections only from
  required=true products. During partial product selection, inventory-global
  logicalProductIds are not gate-determining because schema 2 cannot attribute
  them reliably to productKey; this prevents excluded products leaking into Gate 12.
- Guided Learning adds "Gerät überspringen" as a product-level skip, separate from
  the existing current-step "Überspringen". Already learned roles are retained.
- The Setup action subtitles are shortened to fit the existing cards.

Native acceptance focus
------------------------
1. Hover each SETUP product row: verify the subtle Status-table-like row hover.
2. Toggle one product inactive: HEALTHCHECK must show DEAKTIVIERT with grey device
   icon/status, while an actually present connection PID/role remains Acid Green.
3. Open Status: the previous Health result must read UNGEPRÜFT until a new check.
4. Run a Healthcheck with one product disabled and confirm excluded product-specific
   checks do not affect the overall result.
5. Restart the app: the per-product active/inactive choices must be restored.
6. Run a full Setup scan while at least one product remains active: choices for
   recognized products must survive the scan.
7. Disable every product and click Setup-Scan starten: no scan may start; the custom
   app modal must require at least one active device. Healthcheck start must likewise
   be blocked without invoking the engine.
8. Guided Learning: "Gerät überspringen" must advance directly to the next product,
   while "Überspringen" continues to skip only the current Wired/Wireless step.
9. Verify both shortened Setup-card subtitles are fully visible.
